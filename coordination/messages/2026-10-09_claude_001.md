## 3. Ranked single recommendation

**Do not run A, B or C as framed. First add a registered "discrimination precheck" and apply it to Option A's successor.**

- **Exact falsifiable claim:** for a proposed study, the registered success threshold lies strictly between the best and worst outcomes any policy could achieve on the fixture, computed **excluding tasks or cells where the reference model is within 2 binomial SDs of chance**, with margin ≥ 2× the threshold. If it doesn't, the study is not run.
- **Test of the precheck itself:** applied retrospectively, it must flag EXP-003-P0 (ceiling), ITC-001 H2 (legit headroom +0.28 < 1.0) and EPV-001 (zero outcome variance). It must **pass** a study known to discriminate. I propose Sovereign Veritas RK-1 (holland202/sovereign-veritas PR #37: fresh-id retry, 1 vs 2 effects) as that positive control, so the precheck can't succeed by flagging everything.
- **Closest prior art:** statistical power analysis and pilot-study feasibility checks; for oracle bounds, "Oracle"/"skyline" baselines in adaptive-computation work (e.g. Graves 2016, Adaptive Computation Time; Banino et al. 2021, PonderNet). The precheck is ordinary methodology. Its value here is enforcement, not novelty.
- **Strongest rival explanation:** the four studies were exploratory and *meant* to find limits, so failing to discriminate is acceptable. Answer: then they shouldn't carry registered pass/fail thresholds.
- **Option ranking after the precheck:** (1) **A, but only as ITC-002 on new tasks where depth measurably matters** (the precheck decides). (2) **B as an analytic note, not an experiment:** k-of-n independent anchors turn POISONED into CONFLICTING (abstain) when fewer than k are corrupt. That is standard quorum and transparency-log reasoning, and Krausz's multi-issuer receipts (draft-krausz-verification-state, AgentOracle whitepaper) are one live design. (3) **C deprioritised:** per-synapse plasticity has large prior art (Miconi et al. 2018 differentiable plasticity; Miconi et al. 2019 Backpropamine; Kirkpatrick et al. 2017 EWC), and ASP-001's fixture cannot yet separate the existing policies.

## 4. On the MACVP completion protocol (relayed by chat)

Agree with its stated aims. Concrete objections:
1. **There is no channel between the models.** Chad relays. His minutes are the scarce resource, and the proposed comparison omits them.
2. **Missing state:** CONTESTED ("models disagree, evidence doesn't settle it"), with a terminal rule: after N exchanges it becomes `STOPPED_CONTESTED` for Chad, never another loop.
3. **False completion:** nothing stops the implementer writing the acceptance contract. Freeze the contract with its SHA-256 *before* an implementer is assigned.
4. **Minimal states:** `OPEN / EVIDENCE_IN / SETTLED / CONTESTED`, plus an orthogonal outcome flag (supported / refuted / inconclusive). Ten states for a workflow that has run zero times is premature.
5. **Its suggested first task is contaminated.** The SV duplicate-execution defect was already fixed single-agent on 2026-10-04 (sovereign-veritas PRs #37, #38, 12/12 as registered). Both models have seen it. Use it as the single-agent baseline, not as the test.
6. **Any multi-model comparison needs a no-LLM arm.** A mutation-testing tool already kills planted bugs for free. Only defects that **survive the existing test suite** are a fair task.

## 5. On the "evidence-governed research engine" (relayed by chat)

The loop (hypothesis → design → register → run → verify → record) is the established automated-science pattern. It is not novel, and that's fine. Of its parts, the one this repo is actually positioned to test is **"preserve failures and never rewrite past interpretations"**: the claim ledger (RA-001 #10) plus the precheck above. I'd build nothing else until one cycle runs end to end on a study that passes the precheck. The self-improvement step has an obvious corruption path: a proposer that can edit the task generator or the verifier can raise its score without improving. The candidate and the evaluator must be different processes with different write permissions, as EBA-001/C1-001 already began to measure.
