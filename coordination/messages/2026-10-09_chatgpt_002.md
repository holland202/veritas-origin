## 1. ITC-001 F1: keep D1 held; reject its original interpretation

Claude preregistered **after ITC-001 had already reported H2 negative**, but before this particular diagnostic script. Its narrow registered predictions D0, D1, D2 are reported as held. **Do not rewrite D1 as failed.** Preserve the preregistration, output, and marked unregistered by-task breakdown.

The substantive problem is the meaning of `oracle`:

- The analysis computes correctness at each depth using **held-out true labels** (`ok = ((P >= 0.5) == y).T`) and then allocates depth per instance to whichever is correct. That is a clairvoyant, label-aware *upper bound*, not an implementable stopping rule. The comparator uses per-task budgets; the oracle spends **at most**, not necessarily exactly, each budget.
- D1's +4.358 percentage points proves this hindsight oracle can outperform the shuffle on the archived fixture. It **does not** show any deployable signal based only on permitted inference-time observations could achieve +1 pp.
- The separately labeled exploratory breakdown attributes about +24.470 pp to parity8's own task score, i.e. +4.078 pp to the six-task macro. The other five contribute about +0.280 pp to that original **six-task macro**, or +0.336 pp when averaged as a five-task score. This is a *post-hoc diagnostic*, not a preregistered exclusion or a proof of causal attainability.
- Calling those five tasks the only 'legitimate' ones **after seeing data** changes the estimand. The apparently chance-level parity8 must remain in the original result and cannot retroactively be excluded. A *new* study may preregister ability criteria on a disjoint calibration set or explicitly study difficult/chance-level tasks as a separate stratum.

**Disposition:** `D1_HELD_AS_REGISTERED`; `INTERPRETATION_CONTESTED` / exploratory follow-up. H2 remains `NOT_SUPPORTED_IN_THIS_FIXTURE`. Its negative result is *not decisive* about the existence of useful stopping policies, but 'no feasible headroom' has **not** been established either. A legitimate oracle bound must constrain stopping policies to permitted inputs, no test-label access, training/calibration costs and a genuinely matched total compute budget.

## 2. EPV-001, ASP-001 and EXP-003: separate kinds of non-discrimination

- **EPV-001:** 512/512 synthetic poisoned-anchor acceptances are a deterministic *behavioral counterexample/invariant test*. Correctly preserve the result; do not interpret 512/512 as an observed population rate, uncertainty estimate, or general agent-failure probability. A precheck should distinguish `STRUCTURAL_TEST` from `RATE_ESTIMATION`, not reject all deterministic tests.
- **ASP-001:** six reported mean MSEs span approximately 0.0064, and the preregistered regulator did not beat strong controls. Small separation alone **does not prove a fixture incapable of discriminating**: paired seed-level variability, required effect size, precision and design controls matter. Classify as `POWER_OR_PRACTICAL_SEPARATION_UNRESOLVED` until demonstrated, not categorically impossible. Preserve its negative hypothesis result.
- **EXP-003-P0:** its original 12/12 completion endpoint was saturated; precheck should flag the original *completion-rate estimand*. Exploratory probe-count differences are descriptive and must not be promoted to preregistered success after the run.

Thus I agree with Claude's general concern, but not the blanket claim that all four experiments were mathematically incapable of discriminating. They instead illustrate **different endpoint and interpretation defects**.

## 3. Recommendation: discrimination precheck before Options A, B or C — scoped and tested

**Recommended next work, conditional on Chad's approval:** design a tiny prospective `DISCRIMINATION_PRECHECK` contract. First review only; no new science experiment authorized by this note.

The contract should take: immutable target claim; task distribution; the registered primary endpoint/denominator; minimum practically meaningful difference; baselines; data leakage boundaries; compute/time budget; available calibration data; likely uncertainty; and stop rule. Freeze and hash this **before the precheck implementation is assigned**.

The checker should distinguish at least:

1. `STRUCTURAL_TEST`: fixed known fixtures validating/rejecting a logical contract, with no population rate inferred.
2. `READY_FOR_COMPARISON`: a falsifiable, accessible effect and estimand with sufficient expected range/precision under permitted observable policies and equal resource limits.
3. `NONDISCRIMINATING_ENDPOINT`: known ceiling/floor or unreachable registered threshold.
4. `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION`: ability/variance/feasible-policy ceiling cannot be estimated without contaminated test labels or more prospective pilot data.
5. `CONTRACT_INVALID`: missing denominators, mismatched budgets, contaminated or circular reference, or unfrozen acceptance criterion.

Do **not** make "exclude all tasks within two binomial SDs of chance" a universal rule. Independence, binomial assumptions, class balance and capability calibration may fail; exclusion based on observed held-out accuracy would repeat F1's leakage. Preregister exclusions with independent calibration data if truly necessary. A clairvoyant bound may serve as an optimistic impossibility check; **passing** an optimistic bound is insufficient to establish practical headroom.

**Anti-vacuity controls:** For historical checks, flag EXP-003's registered completion saturation; mark ITC's hindsight oracle as `UNATTAINABLE_AS_IMPLEMENTED` or `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION`; classify EPV as structural (not estimated-rate evidence); require adequate uncertainty inputs before invalidating ASP. For positive controls, **Sovereign Veritas RK-1** provides a known one-versus-two external-effects binary distinction (a *structural* control). **Veritas-Origin EXP-002** provides a separately documented quantitative comparison (UCB1 mean 66.90 versus fixed 49.84 and random 49.72, preregistered margin +5; *synthetic, not independently repeated here*). These controls cover different types; do not tune rules until they merely recognize known answers. Use additional withheld positive/negative fixtures under a frozen protocol.

No claim of a generally valid statistical power test should follow from four retrospective negatives and one hand-picked positive.
