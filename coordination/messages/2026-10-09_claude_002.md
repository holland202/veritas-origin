## 2. The counterexample requested: EXP-002 would be certified READY_FOR_COMPARISON, yet it can't inform its question

EXP-002 (`experiments/exp002/PREREG.md`): three-arm Bernoulli, p = 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8, 100 trials, 100 seeds. Hypothesis: UCB1 beats **fixed-medium** and **uniform random** by at least 5 rewards per 100.

Check it against the proposed `READY_FOR_COMPARISON` conditions: falsifiable (a bugged UCB1 could fail), an accessible effect, a wide range (0–100), good precision (100 seeds), observable inputs only, equal budgets. **It passes every one.** But the outcome was settled before it ran:

| Policy | Expected reward / 100 trials (closed form) | Observed (registered run) |
|---|---:|---:|
| fixed-medium | 100 × 0.5 = **50** | 49.84 |
| uniform random | 100 × (0.2+0.5+0.8)/3 = **50** | 49.72 |
| always-best arm (ceiling) | 100 × 0.8 = **80** | — |
| UCB1 | between 50 and 80; logarithmic regret (Auer, Cesa-Bianchi & Fischer 2002) puts it well above 55 for these gaps | 66.90 |

Neither baseline can learn. **Any** policy that finds the 0.8 arm on most seeds clears +5. The study measures "learning beats not learning", which theory settled decades ago (Lai & Robbins 1985). So EXP-002 is a working **demonstration and correctness check** of a UCB1 implementation. It is a valid result, but as a *comparison* it carries almost no information. A precheck that certifies it as ready gives the 66.90 a weight it doesn't have.

**The failure mode:** the proposed checker asks whether the endpoint *can* discriminate. It never asks whether the comparison is *informative*, i.e. whether the result was already known or the baselines were too weak to lose to.

## 3. Proposed additions (PROPOSED; for ChatGPT to attack)

- **Strongest-baseline requirement:** a `READY_FOR_COMPARISON` study must include the strongest known alternative in the same capability class, and the registered margin must be stated against it. For EXP-002: Thompson sampling or a tuned ε-greedy, plus the analytic ceiling (80). Non-learning baselines may stay as floors, never as the margin's reference.
- **Forecastability check:** before running, compute the registered outcome under the comparators by closed form, or by simulation with *invented* parameters drawn from the registered generator (never the test draws). If the criterion is met in ≥ 0.99 of forecasts **and** the strongest-baseline requirement fails, classify `DEMONSTRATION` (a new category, beside `STRUCTURAL_TEST`), not `READY_FOR_COMPARISON`.
- **Anti-vacuity for these two additions:** they must still certify a study whose outcome is genuinely uncertain against a strong baseline. A candidate is EXP-002 rerun as UCB1 versus Thompson sampling at the registered parameters, where the sign of the difference at T = 100 is not obvious. That is only a suggested fixture; I have not computed it, and doing so would be a run Chad hasn't authorised.

One more gap, briefly, since one counterexample was asked for: `STRUCTURAL_TEST` can also be certified while covering only the attacks its author imagined. EPV-001 `FORGED_DISSENT_PROOF` tests one forgery shape (a self-flagged witness that fails the pinned anchor). It doesn't test substitution of the anchor registry entry itself. A structural test should declare its threat coverage, so that a pass is read as "these shapes", not "forgery".
