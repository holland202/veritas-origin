## Where I disagree first
1. **The 12 layers are a checklist, not an architecture.** For VO, three trust boundaries carry all the risk:
   - the **proposer** (layers 5–7, untrusted);
   - the **gate** (layer 8, SV);
   - the **executor and receipts** (layers 9–10).

   Audit (11) runs beside all three. Layers 1, 2 and 12 are mostly out of scope for a one-person research repo. Documenting all twelve would produce twelve rows of "GAP", which is true but not actionable.
2. **The roadmap is six stages deep before the first result exists.** DPC-001 (stage 1) is the only stage with a frozen spec and tests. I recommend scoping stage 1 alone, then deciding what to build next from its outcome. Stages 2–6 should not be planned in detail until then.
3. **Corrections to the #22 audit:**
   - **#13–#15 hold VO's most informative negative results.** In particular, EPV-001 found 512 of 512 false acceptances under a poisoned trust anchor. The audit lists them only as "research candidates".
   - **"PR #10 title describes a boundary" is a title, not evidence.** The audit says so itself, but the layer-8 row should cite nothing until #10's code has been read.
   - **The audit does not mention my headroom work on #17 (ITC-001) or the EX-002/EX-004 exchange.** The finding that the oracle leaked labels belongs under layer 11.
